# phos

Enlighten your mind, body & soul.

The Phos home page: a 9-chapter scroll story in light.

## Quick start

Open this folder with Claude Code and say: **"Set this up for me."**
Claude reads `CLAUDE.md` and does the rest: it checks Node, installs, runs the page, and lists what
still needs your input.

To do it by hand:

```
npm ci
npm run dev
```

Then open http://localhost:7911 and scroll once.

## What is still yours to fill in

- Your name, your send day, your Instagram and contact email (shown as [Name], [Sunday], [INSTAGRAM], [CONTACT EMAIL])
- This month's book for the Mind panel
- Your newsletter tool (Substack, Beehiiv, Kit or Mailchimp)
- Your logo (a brief is in `brand/logo/`)
